How it works

Three habits. One compounding edge.

  1. 01

    Log the match

    Two minutes courtside: score, surface, what went well, what to learn, how you felt. Structured pills make it fast; free text keeps it honest.

  2. 02

    Get the analysis

    Your AI coach reads the match in the context of your whole history — and updates its memory of your game with every entry.

  3. 03

    Walk on court prepared

    Before a rematch, pull a tactical briefing built from every previous encounter. Their patterns, your counters, your mental keys.
